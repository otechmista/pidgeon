# Specification gate

- Do not write or modify implementation code until `specs/prd.md`, `specs/requirements.md`, and `specs/design.md` are complete and explicitly approved by the user.
- Treat `approved: true` plus a completed `## Approval` section in `specs/prd.md` as the approval signal.
- Never approve specifications, fill approval identity fields, or infer approval from conversational enthusiasm.
- Before implementation, report any missing approval, placeholder, contradiction, or unresolved blocking question and stop.
